wrzucyt – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Who we are

wrzucyt is a private tool operated by Adam Pawłowski for his own use only. Contact: destintoffice@gmail.com

What data the app accesses

With the owner's consent given through Google OAuth, the app accesses the owner's own YouTube account to: upload videos and set their titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails (YouTube Data API), and read channel and video statistics (YouTube Analytics API, read-only).

How the data is used and stored

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and to prepare performance reports for the owner. OAuth tokens and downloaded statistics are stored locally on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

YouTube and Google

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at Google security settings. Locally stored data is deleted by the owner on request or when access is revoked; requests can be sent to destintoffice@gmail.com.

wrzucyt

wrzucyt is a private, single-user tool used by its owner (Adam Pawłowski) to upload his own videos to his own YouTube channels and to read analytics of those channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

This tool uses YouTube API Services (YouTube Data API v3 and YouTube Analytics API), only on the owner's own Google account. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.
